## 3. The creative system: pointed, specific and worth answering

The requested tinge of ragebait comes from challenging a study ritual people defend, then letting a real example settle the argument. Use three repeatable arguments, each with a calm control version:

1. **Recognition versus production:** “That solution looks obvious. Build the next line without it.” Luna conceals the worked answer and reveals a fresh original question.
2. **Score versus explanation:** “A score tells you where you landed. Show me the step.” Luna points to one real observation in the learner's attempt.
3. **Planning versus practising:** “The perfect study plan still needs a first attempt.” Luna swaps a decorative planning prop for the actual Quick Mission screen.

The anger is aimed at a myth, not the viewer. Avoid “you're lazy,” “your child is failing,” diagnoses, humiliating grades, teacher conspiracies, competitor-user insults and before/after academic miracles. The existing anti-slop position is strong enough without invented evidence. “A p-value isn't a permission slip” works as a statistics discussion; “this app guarantees your statistics grade” does not. Medical, nursing, legal and finance examples remain fictional educational practice, reviewed by someone qualified in that material. Exam creatives use original questions, the relevant trademark disclaimer and no official board endorsement; never show confidential or stolen test material.

Give viewers a small prediction to make: identify the invalid algebra line, choose the stronger paragraph reason, or state which assumption a graph needs. Then show the answer honestly. A share invitation can be “Send the study argument, not your homework.” Do not ask people to tag a struggling child, post private work, disclose a diagnosis, or complete engagement tasks to unlock an answer. Human moderation removes exposed personal work and abuse; automation must not diagnose commenters or send unsolicited DMs.

For virality, make Luna's reaction the recurring visual signature and the specific problem the variable. Produce an ordinary explanatory version beside the provocative version. Track useful comments—people explaining a step or asking about practice—separately from outrage volume. Reuse an argument across three related subjects only when each proof clip really matches; changing a label over the same unrelated question is not personalization.

The first three production briefs are concrete: adult algebra opens “Moving a term isn't a reason,” shows an original equality error, the actual feedback and a corrected line; college study opens “The highlighted page isn't a practice attempt,” then shows notes closed, a response and a fresh question; the parent AI/Homework version opens “An answer machine isn't automatically a learning tool,” then shows an eligible teen's prepared attempt before the hint. Each ends “Join the beta waitlist—free.” These are proposed scripts requiring the proof/build/review gates below, not completed recordings or learner testimonials.

The appendix defines the topic, audience, hook, scene, proof brief, CTA and priority for every ICP. **P0** means first review/capture candidates, not automatic publication. **P1** means expand after related proof and qualified outcomes. **P2** means hold for extra policy, audience or domain review. Health-sensitive path labels are preserved in the inventory while their paid variants remain held.

## 6. Automation architecture, with approval attached to the actual asset

Build a local manifest-driven factory, then connect publishing adapters only after separate authorization. This is the proposed system, not an assertion that these jobs already exist:

`ICP record → script/storyboard → claim and rights check → deterministic Luna render + real proof capture → composition/QA → human-approved asset → authorized staging → paused objects/private upload → approved distribution → monitoring/pause`.

The manifest records inventory index, canonical URL, adult/parent role, intended learner age, country, locale, hook/control, Luna source commit/export ID, capture build/API commit, evidence references, licensed voice/music, offer fingerprint, expiry, render hash, destination hash and approval version. Tokens and personal learning content never enter the manifest. Use neutral campaign IDs in UTMs: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content=<immutable-creative-id>` and a non-sensitive test-cell identifier. Do not encode diagnoses, child attributes or private subjects in event payloads or audience lists.

Cache renders by source/model/camera/script/capture hashes. A worker owns a bounded job, records resumable output and retries only transient failures with backoff. A changed claim, example, offer, crop, soundtrack, destination or disclosure invalidates approval. Show the reviewer the final video, captions, thumbnail, landing snapshot and exact audience; an LLM cannot approve its own draft. Domain-sensitive examples require qualified review. Use a dead-letter queue for failures rather than auto-posting a fallback.

Keep credentials in a server-side secret store with allowlisted account IDs, least privilege, expiry checks and audit logs. Creator-posting and ads-management credentials are separate. Idempotency uses account/platform/ICP/locale/creative hash/destination hash/approval version. Store remote IDs and resumable upload sessions. After an ambiguous create timeout, reconcile before retrying; two retries must not create two live campaigns. Never log bearer headers, signed upload URLs or refresh tokens.

Uploading private media and creating paused ads are still external mutations. This planning request authorizes neither. The future staging gate must name account, reviewed asset hashes, visibility/status, quota and allowed actions. The activation gate separately names countries, ages, placements, start/end, currency, per-cell limits and aggregate loss ceiling. Keep local `publish_enabled` and `spend_enabled` controls false until that exact batch is approved. Never automatically unpause after policy rejection, expired approval or a kill event.

## 9. Measurement, replication and unit economics

Track the chain separately: delivered video → qualified landing visit → confirmed free waitlist signup → invited learner → installed starter → completed first mission → proposed useful session → mature return → paid customer. Views, watch time, shares and comments diagnose creative attention. They do not establish learning, activation, retention, revenue or product-market fit. A high-view outrage clip with poor matched-page quality is a losing acquisition asset.

The current [DAU/day-7 growth plan](https://github.com/griffinkrutherford/coherascent-labs-app/blob/main/docs/2026-10-26-dau-nud7-growth-plan.md) defines activation as first-session mission completion. `onboarding_first_question_reached` is earlier. The proposed richer useful session requires an attempt, usable feedback, correction/reasoned follow-up and fresh transfer practice; that metric contract remains separate and must be agreed before dashboards assert it. Initially reconcile existing events with observed consenting sessions. Existing ingestion is at least once, so retries can duplicate rows; first observed account-open is not proof of a new installation. Do not merge learners because a device is shared, or quietly change an app-open cohort into an activated cohort.

A confirmed signup is not automatically a qualified adult lead. Agree the qualification rule using voluntary role/topic information and eligibility confirmation at invitation; ad targeting alone does not prove age. Keep duplicates, internal traffic and invalid submissions visible, and collect only necessary consented fields. Do not manufacture missing qualification data from browsing behavior.

Report exact D1/D7 separately from days 6–8. For the proposed useful-practice return metric, divide returning useful-practice learners by activated learners whose entire window has elapsed; list numerator/denominator and keep immature windows pending. Day-25 entrants cannot all have mature days-6–8 results on day 30. Separate ordinary learners from internal/demo traffic, and segment by creative, page, audience role, locale, platform and build. No private answers, grading text, scans, diagnoses or child records go to ad platforms. Pixel/CAPI installation is a separate privacy/consent change; this document does not install it or assume existing attribution works.

Agree the attribution window and first-party creative/lead linkage before comparisons. Report platform-claimed conversions separately; do not sum overlapping view-through claims across networks as distinct learners.

Replicate a candidate winner with an untouched control: same page/placement/audience/time window, one changed hook. Review lead quality and sampled first sessions before adding an adjacent ICP. Use holdouts or randomized allocation where authorized and feasible; otherwise label cohort comparisons observational. Do not compare a new platform's three-second view definition with another platform's completed view as if equivalent. Pre-register the business decision, loss ceiling and minimum worthwhile effect; small cohorts give directional evidence, not automatic significance. Platform learning guidance never overrides safety or spending caps.

Compute cost per confirmed qualified lead, cost per installed starter, and **CAC to activation** separately from **CAC to paid customer**. Beta access is free; paid CAC/payback may be unobservable. Contribution margin means net receipts after store/payment fees, refunds, model/OCR/voice/retry/review costs, incremental hosting and support labor. Payback is acquisition cost divided by positive monthly cohort contribution, using observed revenue/retention; an upfront annual payment is not twelve months of earned contribution. Owner-set CAC ceilings must follow an approved margin/retention assumption, not a guessed industry benchmark. Count failed generation, support minutes and abandoned starts in the funnel.

Revoke upload/activation jobs, invalidate affected manifests, keep the last accepted creative/landing snapshot, and retain the failure record. Rolling back an ad must also restore its approved destination/offer; it must not silently serve a revised promise. Reports contain rendered asset hashes, account/object IDs, spend freshness, approval versions, measurement limits and the next decision.

The launch package is complete when the 120-row inventory validates, all source/rights/claim/build references resolve, selected final videos pass review, platform staging/stop permissions are demonstrated, budgets are explicitly approved, and ordinary learner support is ready.
