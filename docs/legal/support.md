# Lune Synth Support

Need help? Email [griffin@lunesynth.com](mailto:griffin@lunesynth.com) and
you'll get a reply within 2 business days.

When you write in, it helps to include your device, your OS version, and what
you were doing when the problem happened.

## Signing in

**I didn't get my confirmation email.**
Check your spam or junk folder. Our email comes from mail.lunesynth.com. If it
still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, use "Resend" on the confirmation
screen, or email us.

**I forgot my password.**
Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen. We'll email you an 8-digit code.
Enter that code in the app, then choose a new password.

Note: the email also contains a link, but the link opens our website — it won't
reset your password on its own. Use the code in the app.

**My code says it's wrong or expired.**
Codes are single-use and time-limited. Request a fresh one and enter it without
spaces. If you request several, only the newest one works.

**I was using Lune Synth as a guest and lost my progress.**
Guest sessions aren't tied to an email address, so they can't be recovered or
transferred. Creating an account saves your progress from that point on.

## Using the app

**The app is asking for camera or photo access.**
That's for photographing handwritten work so it can be graded. You can decline
and type or dictate your answers instead.

**The app is asking for microphone access.**
That's for spoken answers. Audio is transcribed and immediately discarded —
recordings are never stored.

**Grading is taking a while.**
Handwriting and multi-step problems take longer than multiple choice. If it's
stuck for more than a couple of minutes, close and reopen the app; your work is
saved.

## Your account and data

**Delete your account:** open the app's settings and choose Delete Account.
Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. You can also request it at
[lunesynth.com/delete-account](/delete-account/).

## Beta testers

Thanks for testing. The fastest way to report something is in the app, using
Send Feedback in Settings. That attaches your app version automatically, which
saves a round trip.

For anything you can't report from inside the app — crashes on launch, sign-in
problems — email [griffin@lunesynth.com](mailto:griffin@lunesynth.com).
