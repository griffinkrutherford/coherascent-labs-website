An example question

Example feedback on a linear ODE

Your work

“y′ + y = x, integrating factor e^x. (e^x y)′ = x e^x, so e^x y = x e^x + C, y = x + Ce^(−x). y(0) = 1 gives C = 1.”

What feedback points to

Rewriting as y′ + y = x and using e^x as the integrating factor are both right. The integral of x e^x needs integration by parts: x e^x − e^x. That gives y = x − 1 + Ce^(−x), and y(0) = 1 makes C = 2.

Luna's hint

Differentiate x e^x. Do you get back x e^x, or something extra?

Questions

Questions from STEM majors

Can it read handwritten math?

Yes. Lune Synth reads handwritten work, including equations, and turns each page into steps it can check. When it isn't confident about what it read, it flags that for review, and you can inspect any step and challenge a deduction you think is wrong.

Which STEM subjects does it cover?

Calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, discrete math, probability and statistics, physics, chemistry, engineering and computer science, among others.

Can I use Problem Solver on graded homework?

That's up to your course. Problem Solver teaches the reasoning step by step rather than printing an answer, but if your course limits outside help on graded work, follow its rules and use Lune Synth on practice problems instead.

Does it check proofs?

Yes. Proof questions get feedback on each step of the argument. The discrete math and PhD qualifying exam pages show examples.
