Questions

Questions about studying in short sessions

Is Lune Synth designed for ADHD?

Lune Synth isn't a medical or clinical product, and it makes no claims about treating ADHD or any condition. It's built around short sessions, quick feedback and one clear next step, which many students find easier to start. For support with ADHD itself, talk to a doctor or your school's disability services office.

Do I have to handwrite my answers?

No. You can write on paper and scan it, type, speak your reasoning and edit the transcript, or answer choice questions. Switch to whichever is easiest to start with that day.

What does the focus timer do?

You pick a session length and Tock, the clock character, counts down with you. You can choose to put Moon Rocks on finishing, with no stake by default. Blocking distracting apps during a session is coming soon.

What happens if I skip a few days?

Your missions and Constellations stay where you left them, and the spaceship button reopens your most recent mission, so getting back in takes one tap.
