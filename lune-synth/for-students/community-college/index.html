The question in the screenshot

Example feedback on a slope question

Your work

“Slope = (6 − 2) / (17 − 5) = 4/12 = 1/3.”

What feedback points to

Taking a difference over a difference is the right method. The change in y goes on top: (17 − 5) / (6 − 2) = 12/4 = 3. Here the run was divided by the rise.

Luna's hint

Slope is rise over run. Which coordinate does the rising, x or y?

Next practice

Three slope questions from two points, then two where you read the points off a graph yourself.

Questions

Questions from community college students

Does it match my college's course?

Lune Synth doesn't connect to your college or its gradebook. It builds practice from the topic you type or the materials you add, such as a syllabus, homework or lecture PDFs, so it follows what your class covers.

Can I use it to prepare for a math placement test?

Yes, as practice. Type the topics your placement test lists, such as linear equations or fractions, and work through them with feedback. Lune Synth isn't affiliated with any placement test, so check your college's site for what yours covers.

How long does a session take?

A Quick Mission takes a few minutes. Constellation missions take longer, and you can stop partway; the spaceship button in the bottom bar reopens your most recent mission.

Does it cover statistics and science prerequisites?

Yes. Lune Synth covers statistics, biology, chemistry, anatomy and physiology, accounting and more, with diagrams such as normal curves, Punnett squares and ledgers where the subject uses them.
