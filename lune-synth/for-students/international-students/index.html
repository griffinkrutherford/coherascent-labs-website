For international students studying in English

You know the subject. Practise explaining it in English.

In a second language you can understand an idea and still struggle to write it the way an exam expects. Lune Synth gives you questions that ask you to explain, Key Terms that define subject vocabulary right next to the question, and feedback on whether your reasoning holds up.

Two things at once

The exam tests the subject, in English.

You might know exactly what happens to a cell in salt water and still lose marks because the answer never said why. Explanation questions are practice for that: you write the reasoning in your own words, then see which part was missing.

The question in the screenshot

Example feedback on a history explanation

Your work

“Opportunity: women could work in factories. Hardship: the work was hard.”

What feedback points to

Filling both columns is the right structure. Each point needs a specific reason. An opportunity: factory wages gave some women money of their own. A hardship: long hours, dangerous machines and lower pay than men.

Luna's hint

Why did a factory job matter to a woman who had never earned a wage? Say what changed for her.

Questions

Questions from international students

Is the app in my first language?

Lune Synth's interface is in English, and this page is about practising your subjects in English, the language your exams use.

Does it help with English itself?

Yes, separately. The English as a second language page covers short dialogues, phonemic transcriptions and vocabulary cards for learning English.

Can I answer by speaking?

Yes. Speak your reasoning, edit the transcript, then submit it for feedback. Typing and handwriting work too.

Which subjects does it cover?

Math and the sciences, history, economics, psychology, writing and more, at levels from school through graduate study. Each subject has its own page.
