Your work

“20 + 0.10m = 35, so 0.10m = 15, m = 1.5 minutes.” The equation is right, but the division slipped.

What feedback points to

Setting Plan A equal to $35 is exactly right. 15 ÷ 0.10 is 150, not 1.5. Check it against the table, where 100 minutes is still under $35.
