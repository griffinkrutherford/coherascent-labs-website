Example feedback on a normal-curve question

Your work

“86 is 2 standard deviations above the mean, and 95% of scores fall within 2 standard deviations, so 5% scored above 86.”

What feedback points to

86 being 2 standard deviations above the mean is right, and 95% within 2 standard deviations is the right rule. The leftover 5% splits evenly between both tails, so only half of it is above 86.

Luna's hint

The empirical rule's leftover 5% is split between both tails of the curve. How much is in just the right tail?
