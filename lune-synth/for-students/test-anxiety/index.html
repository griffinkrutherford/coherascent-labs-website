If your mind goes blank on tests

Make test day the twentieth time you've done this, not the first.

Nerves hit hardest when a problem feels unfamiliar. Lune Synth lets you rehearse ahead of time: work the problem on paper, untimed at first, then see which steps held and which one slipped. Practice scores are just for you, so a wrong answer costs nothing and still teaches you something.

Why studying more doesn't calm the nerves

Knowing it at home and producing it under pressure are different skills.

Rereading notes builds recognition: the page looks familiar, so it feels learned. On a test, nothing is on the page until you write it. Practising recall, producing the answer yourself, is the closer rehearsal, and it's how Lune Synth works: you make the attempt first, then see the feedback.

The question in the screenshot

Example feedback on a slope question

Your work

“The slope is 7, because it's the number on its own.” The graph was not used.

What feedback points to

Reading the equation as y = mx + b is the right move. The 7 is the y-intercept, where the line crosses the y-axis. The slope is the number multiplying x, so it's −2: the line drops 2 for every 1 step to the right.

Luna's hint

In y = mx + b, which letter sits next to x? Find it in y = −2x + 7.

Next practice

Three questions on what a slope means in context, such as a phone battery losing charge each hour, then a timed set of five.

Questions

Questions about practising for tests

Can Lune Synth treat test anxiety?

No. Lune Synth is a study tool, not a treatment. It helps with one part of test stress, not knowing whether you're prepared, by giving you practice and specific feedback. If anxiety is affecting your sleep, health or daily life, talk to a school counsellor or a doctor.

Is practice timed?

Only if you want it to be. Questions have no clock unless you start a focus session with Tock. You can also choose to put Moon Rocks on finishing a session, with no stake by default.

What if I freeze on a question?

Ask Luna for a hint. Luna gives one nudge toward the next step without handing over the answer, so you still finish the problem yourself.

Which tests can I practise for?

Class quizzes and finals, and standardized exams like the SAT and ACT. Type the topic or add your study guide, and Lune Synth builds practice at that level.
