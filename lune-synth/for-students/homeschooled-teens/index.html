For homeschooled teens working independently

Run your own course, with feedback built in.

Homeschooled teens often work through chemistry or precalculus with nobody checking each problem as they go. Lune Synth turns your curriculum into a map of skill missions, checks the work you write, and points to the step that needs another look, so you always know what's done and what's next.

Self-paced cuts both ways

Going at your own pace is easier with a map.

Without a class schedule, it's easy to skip the hard chapter or redo the easy one. A Constellation lays out the course as connected skills, so you can see which ones you've practised, which group tests you've passed and what's left before the cumulative challenge.

An example from chemistry

Example feedback on balancing an equation

Your work

“H₂ + O₂ → H₂O₂. Two hydrogens and two oxygens on each side, so it's balanced.”

What feedback points to

Making the atoms match on both sides is the right goal, and the count is correct. Changing H₂O to H₂O₂ changes the product to hydrogen peroxide, though. Balance with coefficients instead: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.

Luna's hint

You can change how many molecules there are, but not what each molecule is. Which number are you allowed to change?

Questions

Questions from homeschooled students

Is Lune Synth a homeschool curriculum?

No. It works alongside the curriculum you already use, turning its topics into practice with feedback. It doesn't replace your books or your family's plan for the year.

Does it give credits, grades or transcripts?

No. Lune Synth doesn't issue credits, official grades or transcripts. It's a place to practise and get feedback on your work.

Can I prepare for the PSAT, SAT or ACT with it?

Yes. The PSAT, SAT and ACT pages cover exam-style practice, and you can build practice on any test topic.

I'm a parent. Where should I look?

The homeschool page for families covers how Lune Synth fits into a homeschool plan.
