The question in the screenshot

Example feedback on reading codons

Your work

Chose Met-Glu-Leu-Trp. “ATG is Met, GAA is Glu, TTC is Leu, TGG is Trp, and TAA stops.”

What feedback points to

Starting at ATG and stopping at TAA is right, and Met, Glu and Trp are correct. TTC codes for phenylalanine (Phe), not leucine, so the chain is Met-Glu-Phe-Trp.

Luna's hint

Look up TTC again. Which two amino acids have codons starting with TT, and what decides between them?

Is Lune Synth affiliated with the AAMC or a medical school?

No. Lune Synth is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the AAMC or any medical school.

Which pre-med courses does it cover?

General biology, general and organic chemistry, biochemistry, physics, anatomy and physiology, statistics and psychology, each with its own subject page.

Can I practise from my lecture slides?

Yes. Add lecture PDFs, a syllabus or a study guide, and Lune Synth builds a Constellation of practice for the course.

Is this MCAT prep?

It's practice for the courses the MCAT draws on. When you're ready for exam-style practice, the MCAT page covers working through mechanisms, calculations and evidence the way the exam asks.
