If you start strong and then stop

Make studying a little every day easier to start.

Consistency is hard when every session feels like a big decision. Lune Synth makes the next one small: a few-minute mission, a focus timer you start with one tap, and a streak and home-screen widget that keep today's session in sight.

Why routines break

‘Study more’ is too vague to do every day.

Big study plans fall apart after the first missed day. What helps is a small next step and a reason to come back tomorrow. In Lune Synth that's a short mission, a timed session, and a streak you can see.
