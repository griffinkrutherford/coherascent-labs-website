If math stopped making sense a while ago

You're not bad at math. You missed a step.

Feeling behind in math usually comes down to a few foundations that never quite stuck, often fractions. Lune Synth finds the first gap in your own work, rebuilds it with number lines and bar models, and gives you a small problem you can actually finish.

Why it feels like everything is hard

New topics are built on old ones you might have missed.

If fractions never made sense, equations with fractions won't either, and everything after that feels impossible. Lune Synth looks at the work you write and finds the earliest step that breaks. Then you practise that step until it holds, without starting the whole course again.

The question in the screenshot

Example feedback on 5/8 vs 3/4

Your work

“5/8 is bigger because 5 and 8 are bigger than 3 and 4.” The number line was not used.

What feedback points to

Comparing the two fractions is the right goal. Bigger numbers on top and bottom don't make a bigger fraction. Marked on the line, 3/4 sits further from 0 than 5/8.

Luna's hint

The line counts in eighths. How many eighths is 3/4?
