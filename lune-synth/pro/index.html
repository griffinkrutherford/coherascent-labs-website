Lune Synth Pro

Coming soon — not yet available to purchase

Lune Synth is free to use during the beta: photograph or type your work, and Luna grades your actual reasoning step by step. Pro is a paid tier we are building on top of that, for people who want more of it. It has not launched, and we are not naming a launch date, because we do not have one yet.

What Pro is planned to include

These are the benefits we are building toward. None of them exist in the app yet, and details may change before launch.

  • More daily practice. A higher daily limit on the Quick Missions and problem sets Lune Synth generates for you, above what the free plan allows.
  • More handwriting grading. A higher limit on how many handwritten pages Luna can grade per submission set.
  • More time with Luna. A higher daily limit on tutor messages, for when a hint alone is not enough.
  • Full constellation history. Keep and revisit your complete progress map instead of a rolling window of it.

Exact numbers for each of these are still being decided. The Pro screen in the app will show the current allowances before you are asked to pay for anything, and this page will be updated to match once they are set.

Planned price

$12.99 / month, planned

This is the price we are currently planning to launch at. It is not final. Consistent with our Terms of Service, the exact price, renewal term, and cancellation method will be shown to you before a paid subscription ever begins, and nothing is charged to anyone today.

Frequently asked questions

When does Lune Synth Pro launch?

We don't have a public launch date yet. Pro is still being built. Join the beta waitlist and we'll tell you first, before it's announced anywhere else.

How much will Lune Synth Pro cost?

We're planning to launch Pro at $12.99 per month. That price is not final. The exact price, renewal term, and cancellation method will always be shown to you before you are ever charged.

What happens to my Founding 40 or First Light discount?

If you earned the Founding 40 or First Light offer, you keep it under Terms of Service §6. Founding 40 members get two months free then 50% off for as long as they keep the account. First Light signups get 50% off their first three months once Pro launches. Neither is available to new signups today.

Can I cancel a Pro subscription?

Yes. Once Pro exists, cancellation will be available from inside the app at any time, and cancelling stops future charges without deleting your account.

Will Lune Synth stay free to use?

The core practice loop, photographing or typing your work and getting graded feedback, is free during the beta. We have not finalized exactly what stays free once Pro launches, and we will announce that here and in the app before it affects you.
