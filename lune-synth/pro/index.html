A few useful answers

Before you choose.

When can I get Pro?+

Pro is coming soon, and we haven't announced a public launch date. Join the beta waitlist to hear when it's available. Joining the waitlist is free and does not start a subscription.

How much will Pro cost?+

Lune Synth Pro Special Offer is $4.99 per month, billed annually as one $59.88 payment. Pro Monthly is $12.99 per month. Prices are in US dollars. Any applicable tax, renewal terms, and cancellation method will be shown before checkout.

How does the seven-day trial work?+

The Special Offer annual plan is planned to offer seven days free to eligible first-time subscribers, once per account. A payment method is required. After the trial, it renews at $59.88 per year unless you cancel before the trial ends. The monthly plan has no trial. Your eligibility and exact charge date will be shown before subscribing.

Does Pro give me a different grader?+

No. Free and Pro use the same grading system. Pro gives you more capacity. AI feedback can make mistakes; feedback that needs review should be checked before you rely on it.

What happens to my Founding 40 or First Light offer?+

Your existing offer remains governed by Terms of Service section 6. Founding 40 members keep two months free, then 50% off Pro for as long as they keep the account. Eligible First Light signups keep priority beta access and 50% off their first three paid months. The applicable plan and exact discount will be shown before checkout; the annual treatment of First Light is not yet finalized.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. Once subscriptions launch, you can manage or cancel your subscription from the app. Cancellation stops future renewals without deleting your account. Cancel an eligible trial before its end date to avoid the first charge.

Can I keep learning for free?+

Yes. The core practice experience is free during beta. The current Free plan includes up to 5,000 AI actions per day and up to 10 handwritten pages per set. We will explain any changes to Free before they affect you.
