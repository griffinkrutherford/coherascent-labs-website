Lune Synth for Business Statistics

Compute the statistic, then state what it actually supports.

Business-statistics courses test more than the calculation: once you have a correlation coefficient or a p-value, you have to say correctly what it does and doesn’t support. Lune Synth gives you the data or the plotted statistic and checks the conclusion you draw from it, not just the arithmetic.

Where business statistics students get stuck

Computing the number is easy. Describing it correctly is the work.

A correlation of -0.9 is easy to compute from the right formula and easy to describe wrong: calling it weak because the relationship is negative, or treating any strong correlation as proof that one thing causes the other. The number is only useful once you can state, precisely, what it means.
