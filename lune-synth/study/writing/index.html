Lune Synth for Writing

Build the argument yourself, then make every sentence carry it.

Composition courses and essay exams reward a clear claim, reasons that actually support it, and sentences a reader can follow. Lune Synth gives you argument maps and sentence structures to work on, and responds to what you write with one focused revision, without writing it for you.

Where writers get stuck

Good prose does not help if the writer did not make the decisions.

An AI-drafted paragraph skips the part that builds the skill: choosing the claim, seeing the assumption that links evidence to it, and repairing a sentence that buries the point. Mapping the argument and revising your own sentences is slower, and it is what improves the next essay.

Structure you can see

Argument maps and sentence diagrams.

Questions can include a Toulmin map with one element missing, a thesis map from topic to reasons, or a sentence diagram that shows how the words in a sentence depend on each other.

Captured from the Lune Synth app. Generated question sets can include a diagram when one helps; figures go through automated checks against the question before they are shown.
