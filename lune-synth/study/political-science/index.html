Lune Synth for Political Science

Place the position, then explain the mechanism behind it.

Intro political science and government courses test whether you can place an ideology, institution or process correctly relative to the others around it, and explain why it sits there. Lune Synth gives you a real spectrum or process to work through and checks the position and the reasoning behind it.

Where political science students get stuck

Naming the ideology is easy. Placing it relative to the others is the work.

A student can define communism and still misplace it on a spectrum relative to socialism and conservatism, because the definitions are familiar but the relative positioning is not. The same gap shows up with institutional processes: naming checks and balances is easier than explaining which branch checks which.
