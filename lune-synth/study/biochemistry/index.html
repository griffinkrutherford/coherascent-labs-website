Lune Synth for Biochemistry

Work the kinetics, trace the pathway, explain the step.

Biochemistry sits between biology and chemistry: you need the structures from organic chemistry and the regulation logic from biology, applied to pathways with a dozen named steps. Lune Synth sets up the kinetics plot or the pathway with a gap and checks the reasoning you write, not just the enzyme name you recall.

Where biochemistry students get stuck

Naming the pathway is not the same as explaining why a step is rate-limiting.

Students can recite glycolysis or the Krebs cycle in order and still freeze when asked which step is irreversible, what a competitive inhibitor does to Km, or why a pathway's regulation makes sense. Working the kinetics math and filling the gap in a pathway diagram is the skill exams actually test.
