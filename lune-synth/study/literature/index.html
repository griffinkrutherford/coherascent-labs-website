Lune Synth for Literature

Trace what the choice sets in motion, not just what it means.

Literature courses reward more than stating a theme: you have to trace what a specific choice in the text actually causes, step by step, and connect that chain back to the character’s motivation. Lune Synth gives you a real cause-and-effect chart from the text and checks the specific link you fill in.

Where literature students get stuck

Naming the motivation is easy. Tracing its consequence is the work.

A student can list the pressures that drive a character to act, ambition, a prophecy, another character’s taunts, and still lose the thread when asked what that action sets off next. Literary analysis has to follow the chain of cause and effect as closely as the chain of motivation that started it.

What grading notices

Macbeth’s coronation is a real effect in the play. It follows from murdering Duncan directly, not from his later fear of Banquo’s descendants.
