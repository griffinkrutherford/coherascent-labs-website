Lune Synth for Probability

Draw the outcomes, then count them.

Probability errors are usually counting errors: an outcome counted twice, a draw treated as independent when it is not, an overlap left out. A tree or a Venn diagram makes the outcomes visible. Lune Synth shows the diagram, reads your working and names the step.

Where probability students get stuck

The arithmetic was right. The outcomes were not.

A second draw that ignores the first, an overlap counted in both sets, a percentage read from the wrong side of the curve. Written steps show whether the model or the arithmetic slipped.
