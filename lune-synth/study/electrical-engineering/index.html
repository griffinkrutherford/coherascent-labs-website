Lune Synth for Electrical Engineering

Work the circuit, build the gate, trace the field.

Electrical engineering problems hide in the setup: a circuit needs its resistors combined correctly before Ohm's law means anything, a half-adder's logic needs every gate traced, a field-line diagram needs the direction read correctly before you can reason about the force. Lune Synth shows the circuit or gate and checks the reasoning you write, not only the final value.

Where electrical engineering students get stuck

A right final current doesn't mean the circuit was reduced correctly.

Students can apply Ohm's law correctly and still combine resistors the wrong way, or get a logic gate's output right for three of four input rows without noticing the pattern breaks on the fourth. Checking the reduction and the full truth table is what the lab and the exam both test.
