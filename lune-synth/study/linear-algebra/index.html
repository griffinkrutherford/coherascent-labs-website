Lune Synth for Linear Algebra

Track every row operation, find the entry that broke.

Linear algebra is long chains of small arithmetic: a product, a row operation, a cofactor. One wrong entry early on changes everything after it. Lune Synth sets out the matrices, reads your working and names the step.
