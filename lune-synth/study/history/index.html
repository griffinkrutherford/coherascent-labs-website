Lune Synth for History

Get the order right, then argue the cause.

History essays and document questions reward two things: knowing what happened when, and building a causal argument from evidence. Lune Synth gives you timelines and cause-and-effect maps to complete, and responds to the argument you write in your own words.

Where history students get stuck

A polished paragraph can hide an argument with no chronology.

An AI-written essay can sound right while putting effects before causes. Students who cannot place events relative to one another struggle to argue causation at all. Practising chronology and causal links directly is what makes the written argument hold.
