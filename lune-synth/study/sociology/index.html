Lune Synth for Sociology

Trace the loop, then classify what it actually does.

Intro sociology courses test whether you can trace a social mechanism step by step, not just attach a label like “reinforcing feedback” to it. Lune Synth gives you a real causal loop or data pattern and checks whether your classification actually follows from tracing every link.

Where sociology students get stuck

Naming the loop type is easy. Tracing every link is the work.

A loop with two negative links can look balancing at a glance, because a negative link feels like it should cancel something out. Tracing the full loop, link by link, is what actually tells you whether the effect compounds or corrects itself, and two negatives in a row compound just like two positives do.
