Lune Synth for Pharmacology

Work the dose, check the rate, explain the curve.

Pharmacology courses test the same calculation under different names: convert a weight-based order to milligrams, set an IV drip rate, read where a drug sits on a dose-response curve. A right final number can still hide a unit error. Lune Synth keeps your working visible and checks where a calculation, not just an answer, went wrong.

Where pharmacology students get stuck

A correct final dose doesn't mean the unit conversion was sound.

Students can get the right number of milligrams and still have flipped a conversion along the way, or read a dose-response curve backwards and call a more potent drug less effective. Keeping the unit conversion and the curve reasoning visible is what separates a careful calculation from a lucky one.
