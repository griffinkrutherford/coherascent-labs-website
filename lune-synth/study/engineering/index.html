Lune Synth for Engineering

Get the supports, loads and units right before the numbers.

In statics, mechanics and controls, a wrong answer usually starts with a wrong model: a pin treated as fixed, a distributed load placed at the wrong point, a feedback sign flipped. Lune Synth shows the beam or block diagram, reads your equations, and points to the assumption or step to revisit.

Where engineering students get stuck

A final value cannot show whether the model was defensible.

Engineering problems combine idealisations, free-body diagrams, sign conventions and units before any arithmetic happens. When only the final number is checked, a sound method with one slip looks the same as a wrong model. Visible working lets feedback tell them apart.
