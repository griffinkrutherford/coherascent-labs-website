Lune Synth for Economics

Draw the shift, find the numbers, defend the story.

Micro and macro courses grade three things together: the diagram, the calculation and the explanation. Students often get the curve right and the reason wrong, or the reason right and the shortage miscounted. Lune Synth shows the model and checks all three.

Where economics students get stuck

Moving a curve is easy. Defending why it moved is the work.

A price ceiling, a tax, an output gap: each needs the right curve, the right direction, numbers read off the right axis, and an assumption stated out loud. Multiple-choice practice checks only the last step. Written answers show which link is weak.

The models you are tested on

Supply and demand, AD-AS, and the curves between them.

Questions can include market diagrams with price controls or taxes, AD-AS with long-run aggregate supply, and demand curves to move along or shift. The quantity you are asked for is not labelled on the diagram.
