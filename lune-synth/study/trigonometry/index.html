Lune Synth for Trigonometry

Read the angle, find the point, show every step.

Trigonometry depends on a few pictures: the unit circle, the sine and cosine waves, and later polar curves. Mistakes often come from a sign in the wrong quadrant or degrees mixed with radians. Lune Synth shows the picture, reads your working and names the step.

Where trigonometry students get stuck

The identity was right. The quadrant was not.

cos(π) written as 1, a reference angle used without its sign, a calculator left in degrees. Written steps show whether the idea or the bookkeeping slipped.
