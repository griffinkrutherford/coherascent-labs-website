Lune Synth for Pre-Algebra

See the model, write the steps, find the slip.

Pre-algebra is where arithmetic turns into algebra: negative numbers, fractions, the distributive property and a first variable. Most mistakes are small and early, like a dropped sign or a fraction flipped the wrong way. Lune Synth shows the model, reads your working and names the step.

Where pre-algebra students get stuck

The idea was fine. One sign or fraction step was not.

A negative sign lost while distributing, 2/3 ÷ 1/6 computed upside down, an open circle drawn where the endpoint is included. Each one makes the final answer wrong for a different reason, and written steps show which.
