Lune Synth for Statistics

Shade the curve, compute the probability, say what it means.

Statistics courses test whether you can set up the right calculation and interpret it: which tail to shade, which z-score to use, what a confidence interval does and does not say. Lune Synth shows the distribution and checks both the setup and the interpretation.

Where statistics students get stuck

A correct calculation can support the wrong conclusion.

Reading the wrong tail from a z-table, comparing medians when the question asks about spread, describing a 95% interval as a 95% chance the mean is inside it: the arithmetic can be flawless and the conclusion wrong. Written setup and interpretation make those errors visible.

Distributions you can see

Normal curves, box plots and sampling distributions.

Questions can include a normal or t curve with the region shaded, side-by-side box plots to compare, or a sampling distribution for a confidence interval. Probabilities and interval ends are left for you to find.
