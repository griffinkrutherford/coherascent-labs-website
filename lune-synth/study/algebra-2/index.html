Lune Synth for Algebra II

Connect the equation to its graph, one step at a time.

Algebra II asks you to move between an equation, its graph and its behaviour: end behaviour, zeros, sums of series, feasible regions. When an answer is wrong, the error can sit in the algebra or in how the graph was read. Lune Synth shows the graph, reads your working and names the step.

Where Algebra II students get stuck

The graph said one thing. The algebra said another.

A leading term read with the wrong sign, a series summed one term too far, a corner point checked against the wrong constraint. Written steps show whether the mistake was in the algebra or in reading the picture.
