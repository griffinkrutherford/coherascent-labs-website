Lune Synth for Algebra

Find the line where your algebra stopped being true.

In Algebra 1 or 2, one sign error or a step done to only one side can undo a page of correct work, and the answer key only tells you the result was wrong. Write your steps, and Lune Synth points to the first line that no longer follows from the one before.

Where algebra students get stuck

The setup was right. One move broke it.

Algebra is a chain of moves that must keep both sides equal: distribute, combine, isolate, check. Learners rarely miss the whole idea. They drop a negative, divide only one term, or forget that multiplying an inequality by a negative flips it. Seeing exactly which move failed is what makes the next problem go differently.
