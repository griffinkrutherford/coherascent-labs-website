Lune Synth for Nursing

Set up the dose, check the units, explain the priority.

Nursing programmes test dosage calculations with no room for error and clinical questions that hinge on the rationale. Lune Synth gives you weight-based doses, IV drip rates and prioritisation questions as educational practice, and checks each step of your setup.

Where nursing students get stuck

The arithmetic is simple. The setup is where errors hide.

Most dosage-calculation mistakes come from skipping a conversion, such as pounds to kilograms or hours to minutes, or dividing a daily dose by the wrong number of doses. Showing the setup with units lets feedback point at the exact factor. Clinical questions work the same way: the rationale matters as much as the choice.
