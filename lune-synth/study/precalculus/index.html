Lune Synth for Precalculus

Know what the function does before calculus asks.

Precalculus is about behaviour: where a function blows up, how a path moves, what a sequence does next. Errors often come from reading one side of an asymptote or plugging into the wrong formula. Lune Synth shows the function, reads your working and names the step.

Where precalculus students get stuck

The formula was right. The behaviour was misread.

An asymptote approached from the wrong side, a projectile's peak taken at the wrong time, a sequence indexed from 0 instead of 1. Written steps show which.
