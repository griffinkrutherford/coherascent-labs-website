Lune Synth for Physics

Read the circuit, the rays or the forces, then write the equation.

In mechanics, circuits and optics, the equation comes after the picture: which resistors are in parallel, where the focal points sit, which forces have a horizontal component. Lune Synth draws the setup, reads your working and points to the step where the model or the algebra went wrong.

Where physics students get stuck

A correct-looking number can hide the wrong model.

Treating parallel resistors as series, using the object distance as the image distance, counting the normal force in the horizontal equation: these are modelling errors, and the final answer alone cannot reveal them. Visible working lets feedback separate the physics from the arithmetic.
