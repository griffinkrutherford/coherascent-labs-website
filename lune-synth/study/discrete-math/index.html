Lune Synth for Discrete Math

Trace the logic one row, edge or state at a time.

Discrete math is precise bookkeeping: one row of a truth table, one edge of a graph, one transition of a machine. A single wrong case breaks the argument. Lune Synth sets out the table, graph or machine, reads your working and names the step.

Where discrete math students get stuck

The argument was right except for one case.

An implication with a false hypothesis marked false, two adjacent vertices given the same color, a transition followed from the wrong state. Written steps show which case broke.
