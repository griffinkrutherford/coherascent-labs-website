Lune Synth for Accounting

Post it, balance it, and explain why it belongs there.

Whether you are in Principles of Accounting or an intermediate course, the hard part is the same: deciding which accounts a transaction touches, on which side, and in which period. Lune Synth gives you journal entries and T-accounts to complete by hand, then points to the first line where your books stop telling the truth.

Where accounting students get stuck

Debits equal credits, and the entry is still wrong.

Most accounting mistakes balance. A prepaid expense recorded in full on the purchase date, revenue recognised before it is earned, a payment posted to the wrong side of a liability: the trial balance agrees and the statements are wrong. Reading a solved example does not train that judgment, because the hard choices are already made. Completing the entry yourself does.
