For parents of teens preparing for the SAT, ACT or PSAT

Test prep that finds the questions your teen keeps missing.

A full practice test every weekend takes hours, and the score alone doesn't say why points were lost. Lune Synth works in short sets of SAT-, ACT- and PSAT-style questions. It reads the working your teen writes and tells a misread from a skill gap, so the next session goes where the points are.

What a practice score hides

Two wrong answers can have two different causes.

One miss is a misread question. Another is a skill that was never solid. A practice score counts them the same. Lune Synth looks at the work behind each answer, so rushing gets a different next step from a real gap, and limited prep time goes to the skills that will change the result.

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

Are these official SAT, ACT or PSAT questions?

No. They are practice questions written for Lune Synth in the style of each test. Lune Synth is not affiliated with the College Board or ACT. For full-length timed practice and the current test format, use the official materials from the organisations that run each exam.

Does Lune Synth predict my teen's score?

No. It doesn't produce a predicted score. It shows which skills are solid and which need practice, based on your teen's own answers.

How much time does it take each week?

That's up to your family. A Quick Mission takes a few minutes, so short weeknight sessions are realistic alongside schoolwork, with longer timed practice when there's time.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
