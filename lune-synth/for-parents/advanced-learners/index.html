The question in the screenshot

Example feedback when the answer is right but the reasoning isn't

Your teen's work

“f′(x) = 2x − 4, so the tangent line is y = 2x − 4.” The final line matches the answer, but it's the derivative, not the tangent.

What feedback points to

The derivative is correct. The tangent needs one slope, f′(3) = 2, and a line through P(3, 2): y − 2 = 2(x − 3). That also simplifies to y = 2x − 4, which is why the answer matched here by coincidence.

Luna's hint

The derivative gives a slope for every x. Which single slope does the tangent at P need?

Next practice

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

Can my teen study a subject their school doesn't offer?

Yes. Your teen can describe any topic, or attach a syllabus or lecture notes, and build practice or a Constellation from it.

How advanced can the material get?

Lune Synth covers material from early math through university courses such as multivariable calculus, linear algebra and discrete math, along with sciences, computer science, languages and writing.

Will it just confirm right answers?

No. Feedback looks at the reasoning as well as the result, so a correct final answer reached by a flawed method still gets a note on the step that doesn't hold up.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
