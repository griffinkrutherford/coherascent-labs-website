Lune Synth reads handwritten math, finds the exact step that broke down, and gives your child a manageable next move—so homework can build independence instead of conflict.

When ‘I don't get it’ is all you hear

A child can struggle with one step and start believing they are bad at math.

The hard part is often locating the gap. A final answer cannot show whether your child misunderstood the idea, lost a sign, or simply rushed. Lune Synth starts with the page they wrote, preserves what they did correctly, and narrows the problem to something they can actually repair.
