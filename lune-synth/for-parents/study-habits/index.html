For parents thinking about focus and screen time

Make some of the screen time count.

The phone is where the study app lives, and also where every distraction lives. Lune Synth keeps a session small and bounded. Tock, the focus timer, counts down a length your teen picks, a Quick Mission takes a few minutes, and whatever your teen writes gets feedback.

Why ‘go study’ doesn't work

A vague goal loses to a notification.

‘Study for an hour’ has no clear start or finish, and it competes with everything else on the phone. A small, defined task is easier to begin: one timer, one mission, one thing to finish. Lune Synth breaks studying into pieces that size, and each one ends with feedback rather than more scrolling.

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

Isn't this just more screen time?

It is time on a screen, so it's worth deciding together how it fits. The difference is what the time is spent on: your teen produces an answer, gets feedback and fixes it. They can also work on paper and photograph the page.

Are there rewards in the app?

Yes. Your teen can earn Cosmic Medals and unlock outfits for Tock as they study. These are in-app collectibles, and the focus timer works the same without them.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
