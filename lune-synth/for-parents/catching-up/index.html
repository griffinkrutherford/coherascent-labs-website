For parents of a teen who has fallen behind

Find the earlier skill that's holding everything up.

A bad grade in algebra might really be about fractions from two years ago. Starting the course over takes too long and can feel like a punishment. Lune Synth reads the work your teen writes, finds the earlier skill that keeps breaking and practises just that, while class keeps moving.

Why catching up feels impossible

Behind in everything usually means a few missing pieces.

Missed weeks, a move, an illness or a class that went too fast can leave holes that later topics depend on. Redoing the whole course takes too long. Fixing the right few skills is faster, and your teen's mistakes show which ones they are.

Example feedback on a fraction gap

Your teen's work

Marked 5/8 correctly, put 3/4 at the third tick, and said 5/8 is greater.

What feedback points to

5/8 is in the right place. The line is split into eighths, so 3/4 is the same as 6/8 and belongs at the sixth tick, not the third.

Luna's hint

How many eighths make one quarter? Count that many ticks, three times.

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

How does it know which skill is missing?

Feedback on your teen's work names the step that went wrong. When the same kind of step keeps going wrong, that's the skill to practise. Lune Synth doesn't run a formal placement or diagnostic test.

Does it replace extra help from school?

No. It's practice with feedback. If your teen's school offers extra help, that's worth taking, and Lune Synth can add practice in between.

Is it only for math?

No. Math is often where gaps are easiest to see, but your teen can practise any subject they describe, from chemistry to Spanish.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
