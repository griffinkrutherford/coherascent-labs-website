For parents weighing tutoring

Help on the nights a tutor can't be there.

A tutor might see your teen for an hour a week, but homework happens on the other nights too. Lune Synth covers those nights. Your teen can photograph the step they're stuck on and ask Luna about it, get a hint rather than the answer, and practise the skill in a short mission.

What good tutoring does

Someone looks at the actual work and asks the right question.

Much of good tutoring comes down to two things: someone reads what your teen actually wrote, and asks the question that gets them unstuck without taking over. Lune Synth is built around those two moves. It isn't a person and doesn't know your teen the way a good tutor does, but it's there whenever the homework is.

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

Does Lune Synth replace a tutor?

It does part of what a tutor does: it responds to your teen's own work and gives hints instead of answers. It doesn't know your teen the way a person can, and like any feedback it can be wrong. If your teen has a tutor, it covers the nights between sessions. If not, it gives feedback that otherwise wouldn't be there.

Will Luna just give the answer if my teen asks?

Luna is built to refuse direct answers. It responds with hints, key terms and questions based on your teen's attempt.

What does it cost?

Joining the waitlist is free. Current plans are on the pricing section of the home page.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
