For parents of teens who learn differently

More than one way to show what they know.

Some teens can explain a solution clearly out loud but stall in front of a blank page. Others need to see a diagram, or lose focus after ten minutes. Lune Synth lets your teen answer by hand, by typing or by speaking, keeps sessions short, and gives one piece of feedback at a time.

When the format gets in the way

Sometimes the hard part is getting the answer down, not the subject.

A teen who understands the material can still struggle if every answer has to be written out at length, or if a page of corrections arrives at once. Lune Synth gives your teen choices about how to answer and keeps feedback to one point at a time, which makes it easier to tell what they know from what they find hard to get down.

Example feedback on a spoken answer

Your teen's spoken answer

“Each parent gives a B or a b. Three of the four squares have a big B, so three quarters are white.”

What feedback points to

The square is filled in correctly. Only bb gives white flowers, so the three squares with a capital B are purple, and the white ones are the one square left.

Which square has no capital B in it? What colour is that flower?

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

Is Lune Synth designed for a specific learning difference?

No. It's a general study tool with flexible ways to answer and short sessions. It isn't a diagnostic, therapeutic or medical tool, and it doesn't replace an IEP, a 504 plan, accommodations from your teen's school, or advice from their teachers and specialists.

Can my teen answer out loud?

Yes. Your teen can speak an answer, check and edit the transcript, and then send it for feedback.

Can my teen work on paper instead of the screen?

Yes. They can work on paper and photograph the page, and feedback responds to that work.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
