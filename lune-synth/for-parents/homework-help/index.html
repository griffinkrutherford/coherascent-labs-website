The question in the screenshot

Example feedback on 23 × 14

Your child's work

200, 30, 80 and 7 → 317. Three of the four parts are right.

What feedback points to

10 × 20, 10 × 3 and 4 × 20 are right. The bottom-right box should be 4 × 3, and it was written as 4 + 3.

Luna's hint

Each box is the length times the width of its rectangle. What are the two sides of the small box?
