For parents of teens with essays and reading due

Writing help that leaves the words to your teen.

A thesis that doesn't take a side, a quotation that just sits there, a conclusion that repeats the introduction. You can tell something is off but not always say what. Lune Synth lays the argument out as a map, reads the paragraph your teen wrote and names the one revision that would help most. The sentences stay theirs.

The red-pen problem

‘It needs to flow better’ isn't something a teen can act on.

Marking up a draft line by line tends to turn into a rewrite in your voice, and general advice is hard to use. Writing improves when the writer can see the structure: the claim, the reason that supports it and the assumption that links the two. Lune Synth works on that structure first, then on the sentences, and gives one change at a time.

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

Will Lune Synth write my teen's essay?

No. Luna, the in-app tutor, gives hints, key terms and questions about the draft, and is built to refuse requests for a finished answer. Feedback responds to what your teen wrote, so the essay stays in their words.

Does it help with reading, or only writing?

Both. Questions can quote a short passage or poem and ask what a line suggests or how a writer makes a point. Your teen answers in their own words, and feedback checks whether the answer is supported by the text.

Does my teen have to type everything?

No. They can type, write by hand in the app, photograph a page written on paper, or speak the answer and edit the transcript before sending it.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
