For parents worried about AI and homework

AI that asks for your child's work before it helps.

A chatbot can write tonight's homework in seconds. Lune Synth works the other way. Your child writes an answer first, by hand, by typing or out loud. The feedback is on that reasoning, and the hints point to a next step rather than a finished answer.

The real worry

Copied answers look like finished homework. Your child hasn't learned anything.

When AI produces the answer, the homework is done and your child has learned nothing from it. Lune Synth uses AI only after your child has made an attempt. Questions ask them to explain the method, and the feedback is on what they actually wrote.
