The Uniform CPA Examination

Three Core sections and one Discipline of your choice.

Since the CPA Evolution changes in January 2024, every candidate takes three Core sections and chooses one of three Discipline sections. Each section is four hours and mixes multiple-choice questions with task-based simulations.

Core
Auditing and Attestation (AUD), Financial Accounting and Reporting (FAR), and Taxation and Regulation (REG).
Discipline
One of Business Analysis and Reporting (BAR), Information Systems and Controls (ISC), or Tax Compliance and Planning (TCP).
Scoring
Each section is scored from 0 to 99, and 75 is the passing score in every jurisdiction.
Common weak spots
Accruals and deferrals booked to Cash, and multi-step calculations where one early figure carries an error into every later line.

Questions shown are Lune Synth practice questions captured in the app, not official CPA Exam items. Exam format is summarised from the AICPA's and NASBA's published CPA Exam information; check aicpa-cima.com and nasba.org for current details.

Questions

CPA Exam prep questions

Does Lune Synth replace a CPA review course?

No. It is a practice and feedback tool you can use alongside a review course: you attempt a problem, and it responds to the specific work you produced.

Which sections does it help with?

It is most useful wherever you have to produce work rather than recall a fact: entries and adjustments in FAR, calculations in REG and TCP, and analysis questions in BAR. You choose the topic for each practice set.

Are these official AICPA questions?

No. Lune Synth writes its own practice questions and is not affiliated with the AICPA, NASBA or any state board of accountancy.

Can I photograph my own work?

Yes. Handwritten work can be scanned with your phone camera, and you can also type or speak an answer.
