English pronunciation attempt

Asked to transcribe the verb "live" from a minimal-pair list, the learner writes /liv/, which matches "leave" rather than the short-vowel verb form.

What grading notices

The transcription format is correct. The vowel sound itself is the piece that changes the word.

Luna's hint

"Live" the verb rhymes with "give," not with "leave." Which transcription matches that short vowel?
