An admissions test built on reasoning, not memorization

A missed ISEE question should point to a skill, not a verdict on the student.

The ISEE leans on quantitative and verbal reasoning rather than rote recall, so a wrong answer often comes from a reasoning step rather than a missing fact. Lune Synth reads the written attempt so a family can see exactly which reasoning step needs another pass, with feedback that stays encouraging for a student.

Quantitative reasoning attempt

Asked which is greater, 5/8 or 3/4, on a blank eighths number line, the student marks both points correctly but reads 5/8 as the larger value.

What grading notices

Both fractions are placed correctly on the number line. The comparison itself is the step that needs a second look.

Luna's hint

3/4 is the same as how many eighths? Compare that to 5/8 on your own number line.

Targeted next practice

Next mission: four more fraction-comparison questions on a blank number line.
