The digital SAT

Two adaptive modules, Reading and Writing, then Math.

The SAT is taken on a computer through Bluebook. Each section's second module adjusts in difficulty based on how the first module went, and a graphing calculator (Desmos, built into the test) is allowed throughout Math.

Reading and Writing
54 questions in 64 minutes, short passages with one question each. Half the score.
Math
44 questions in 70 minutes, with Desmos available the whole time. Half the score.
Format
Adaptive by module: how you do on the first module shapes the difficulty of the second.
Common weak spots
Transition words that need the logic read, not guessed, and word problems translated into the wrong equation.
