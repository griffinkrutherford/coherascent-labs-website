COMLEX-USA

A three-level series for osteopathic licensure.

The NBOME's COMLEX-USA series runs from the preclinical years into residency. Each level is a computer-based, full-day exam built around patient presentations and osteopathic principles and practice.

Level 1
Foundational biomedical science applied to patient care, reported as pass or fail.
Level 2-CE
Clinical knowledge and decision-making for supervised practice, reported with a three-digit score.
Level 3
A two-day exam for unsupervised practice that adds clinical decision-making cases.
Common weak spots
Reflex and feedback loops traced in the wrong direction, and flow pathways where one structure is skipped.

Student attempt

Says carotid baroreceptors detect a fall in blood pressure and that the reflex lowers heart rate in response.

What grading notices

The receptor and its location are right. The response is reversed: a fall in pressure reduces vagal tone and raises sympathetic output, so heart rate rises.

When baroreceptor firing drops, which autonomic arm is released?

Questions

COMLEX-USA prep questions

Does Lune Synth cover osteopathic manipulative medicine?

You can set osteopathic principles and practice as a topic and explain reasoning in writing or by voice. Hands-on technique still has to be learned and assessed in person.

Are these official NBOME questions?

No. Lune Synth writes its own practice questions and is not affiliated with the NBOME.

Can I use it alongside a question bank?

Yes. Use your question bank for timed volume, then use Lune Synth to explain the questions you missed and practise the mechanism behind them.

Does it help with USMLE too?

Many DO students sit both. The mechanism-first habit carries over, and there is a separate USMLE page with Step 1 examples.
