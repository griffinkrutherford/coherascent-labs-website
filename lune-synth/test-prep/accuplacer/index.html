ACCUPLACER-style attempt

The learner graphs x > 2 on the number line but fills in a closed circle at 2 instead of an open one.

What grading notices

The direction of the ray is correct. The circle at the boundary needs to be open because 2 itself does not satisfy x > 2.

Luna's hint

Does the inequality include the boundary value, or exclude it? That decides open versus closed.
