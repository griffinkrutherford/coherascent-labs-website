AP Art History with Lune Synth

Point to the feature, then explain what it does.

AP Art History free response rewards specific visual evidence tied to a claim, such as naming a structural feature and explaining what it allowed, not a general impression of a work. Practice that with feedback on your reasoning.

Where Art History points go missing

"It's impressive" is not visual evidence.

Comparison essays reward naming a specific feature (a material, a form, a structural choice) in each work and explaining what it enabled or signified, not a general reaction to the image.

The AP Art History exam

Image-based multiple choice, then six free-response essays.

The free-response section includes essays on works you must identify from memory as well as unknown works, so specific visual evidence is what separates scores.

Multiple choice
80 questions in 1 hour, mostly image-based. 50% of the score.
Free response
6 essays in 2 hours: two long essays (a comparison, and a visual/contextual analysis) and four shorter essays, three using works outside the provided image set. 50% of the score.
Common weak spots
General impressions instead of named visual evidence, and comparisons that describe each work separately instead of connecting them.

Your practice attempt

Hagia Sophia uses pendentives like the Pantheon uses its dome.

What grading notices

Pendentives is the right term. The stronger answer explains what they do: let a round dome rest on a square base, which the Pantheon's drum-supported dome never needed to solve.

Luna's hint

The Pantheon's dome sits on a round drum; what shape is Hagia Sophia's base?
