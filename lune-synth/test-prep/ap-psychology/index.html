AP Psychology with Lune Synth

Apply the concept, don't just define it.

AP Psych free response grades whether you can apply a concept to a scenario you haven't seen and design or critique a study, not recite a definition. Practice that application with feedback on your reasoning.

Where AP Psych points go missing

Knowing the term is the easy half of the question.

Both FRQs hand you a scenario and ask you to apply multiple concepts to it, or to design and evaluate a study. Students who can define reinforcement, memory, or a research method still lose points when the concept isn't tied to the specific scenario given.

The AP Psychology exam

Mostly multiple choice, with two applied free responses.

The free-response section has two questions: an Article Analysis Question and an Evidence-Based Question built from provided data.

Multiple choice
75 questions in 1 hour 30 minutes. About two-thirds of the score.
Free response
2 questions in 1 hour 10 minutes: an Article Analysis Question and an Evidence-Based Question. About one-third of the score.
Common weak spots
Defining a term without applying it to the scenario, and confusing correlation with causation in research-design questions.

Questions shown are practice questions written for Lune Synth and rendered in the app, not official AP items. Exam formats are summarised from the College Board's course and exam descriptions; check AP Central for current details.
