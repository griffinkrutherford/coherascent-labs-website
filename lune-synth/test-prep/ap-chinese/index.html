AP Chinese Language and Culture with Lune Synth

The tone is part of the word, not an accent on top of it.

AP Chinese scores tone as part of correct pronunciation, since the wrong tone can turn one word into a different one entirely, such as 买 (to buy) into 卖 (to sell). Practice tone pairs with feedback on your Chinese.

Where Chinese points go missing

Right syllable, wrong tone, different word.

Speaking and listening tasks lose points when a tone shift changes the meaning of what you said, not just how it sounds, because Mandarin tones are part of a word's identity.

The AP Chinese Language and Culture exam

A computer-based exam covering reading, listening, writing, and speaking.

The AP Chinese exam is administered on computer and includes typed or handwritten responses as well as recorded speaking tasks, all in Mandarin.

Interpretive
Multiple choice from reading and audio sources. About half the score.
Presentational writing
A message reply and a story narration based on picture prompts.
Presentational speaking
A Project Presentation on independent research and a Project Q&A answering four pre-recorded questions.
Common weak spots
Tone shifts that change a word's meaning, and character writing that drops a required radical.

A tone habit

Trace the tone's shape, not just its pitch.

Your practice attempt

买 sounds like it goes up, so it's 2nd tone.

What grading notices

买 (mǎi) is 3rd tone: it dips down and then rises, which is different from 2nd tone's single rise from low to high.

Luna's hint

Does the tone mark dip down before it rises, or rise the whole way?
