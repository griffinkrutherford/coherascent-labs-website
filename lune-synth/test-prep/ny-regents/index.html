Geometry Regents attempt

In right triangle ABC with legs 6 cm and 8 cm, the student adds the legs directly to get 14 cm for the hypotenuse instead of applying the Pythagorean theorem.

What grading notices

The triangle and the given legs are read correctly. The relationship between the legs and the hypotenuse is the step that needs correction.

Luna's hint

The legs do not add directly to the hypotenuse. What does a² + b² = c² give you with 6 and 8?
