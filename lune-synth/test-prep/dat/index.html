The Dental Admission Test

Four tests in one sitting, scored from 1 to 30.

The DAT is a computer-based exam that covers the natural sciences, spatial reasoning, reading and quantitative reasoning in a single appointment.

Survey of the Natural Sciences
100 questions in 90 minutes: biology, general chemistry and organic chemistry.
Perceptual Ability
90 questions in 60 minutes on spatial reasoning, including angle ranking, hole punching and cube counting.
Reading and Quantitative
50 reading comprehension questions in 60 minutes, then 40 quantitative reasoning questions in 45 minutes.
Common weak spots
Organic naming and functional groups, strand direction in molecular biology, and stoichiometry with a missed unit.

Questions shown are Lune Synth practice questions captured in the app, not official DAT items. Exam format is summarised from the American Dental Association's published DAT information; check ada.org for current details.

Student attempt

Names CH3CH2CHO as propanone and calls its functional group a ketone.

What grading notices

Three carbons is right. The carbonyl is at the end of the chain, which makes it an aldehyde: propanal.

Luna's hint

Is the C=O carbon bonded to one other carbon or to two?

Questions

DAT prep questions

Does Lune Synth cover the Perceptual Ability Test?

Lune Synth is strongest on the sciences, reading and quantitative reasoning, where you can write out your reasoning. Use dedicated PAT practice for the spatial item types.

Are these real DAT questions?

No. Lune Synth writes its own practice questions and is not affiliated with the American Dental Association.

Can I study organic chemistry mechanisms?

Yes. You can draw a mechanism on paper, photograph it and get feedback, and practice questions can show molecular structures.

How does it choose what I practise next?

After each attempt, feedback names the gap. A quick mission then gives you a few more questions on that skill.
