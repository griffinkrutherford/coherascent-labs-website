The Fundamentals of Engineering exam

A computer-based exam in one of seven disciplines.

NCEES offers the FE year-round at approved test centers. You choose the discipline closest to your degree, and the NCEES FE Reference Handbook is the only reference you get, on screen.

Format
110 questions in a six-hour appointment, which includes a tutorial and a scheduled break.
Disciplines
Chemical, Civil, Electrical and Computer, Environmental, Industrial and Systems, Mechanical, or Other Disciplines.
Reference
The searchable NCEES FE Reference Handbook on screen. No personal notes or books.
Common weak spots
Distributed loads placed at the wrong point, series and parallel combinations misread, and unit conversions dropped mid-problem.

Questions shown are Lune Synth practice questions captured in the app, not official FE exam items. Exam format is summarised from NCEES's published FE exam information; check ncees.org for current details.

What grading notices

Summing moments about B is the efficient move. A uniform load's resultant acts at the middle of its span, 3 m from B, which gives R_A = 14 kN.

Questions

FE exam prep questions

Which FE discipline does Lune Synth cover?

You choose the topic for each practice set, so you can focus on your discipline's subjects, from statics and circuits to thermodynamics and fluids. It is strongest on problems you can set up and work on paper.

Are these official NCEES questions?

No. Lune Synth writes its own practice questions and is not affiliated with NCEES. Use the NCEES practice exam for your discipline as well.

Can I use the FE Reference Handbook with it?

Yes. Practising with the handbook open is a good habit, because it is the only reference you get on exam day. Lune Synth checks the setup you write, whatever reference you used.

Does it help with the PE exam?

The same habit of setting up before solving carries over, and you can set PE-level topics for practice sets. This page focuses on the FE.
