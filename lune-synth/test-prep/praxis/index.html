Content knowledge for teaching

Getting the answer is not the same as knowing why it works.

Future teachers sit Praxis tests on material they last studied years ago, and many subject tests ask how a concept works, not just what the answer is. Area models, place value and the structure of an argument all reward explaining. Writing the explanation shows whether you understand the method or only remember the steps.

The Praxis tests

Core skills plus subject tests set by your state.

ETS's Praxis tests are used for teacher licensure in many states. Which tests you take, and the passing scores, depend on your state and the subject you will teach.

Praxis Core: Math
Number and quantity, algebra and functions, geometry, and statistics and probability, with an on-screen calculator.
Praxis Core: Reading and Writing
Reading comprehension and argument questions, plus essays in the Writing test.
Subject Assessments
Content tests for what you will teach, from Elementary Education to secondary math, science and English.
Common weak spots
Partial products missing from multi-digit work, and passages where a counterargument is mistaken for the claim.

Questions

Praxis prep questions

Which Praxis tests does Lune Synth help with?

It is most useful for Praxis Core Math and Writing and for subject tests where you solve problems or explain concepts, such as elementary education, mathematics and the sciences. You choose the topic for each practice set.

Are these official ETS questions?

No. Lune Synth writes its own practice questions and is not affiliated with ETS. Use ETS's free practice materials as well.

Can it give feedback on essays?

It can give feedback on short written responses, such as whether a paragraph states a claim, supports it and answers a counterargument. It does not predict an official Praxis essay score.

Do I need to know which tests my state requires?

Yes. Requirements and passing scores vary by state, so check your state's licensure office and ETS before you plan your study.
