Where Physics 2 points go missing

Signs in the first law and areas on a P-V graph.

Students mix up work done by and on a gas, forget that internal energy returns to its start after a cycle, and describe ray diagrams or field lines without connecting them to the equation.

The AP Physics 2 exam

The same four free-response skills, new content.

AP Physics 2 uses the same free-response question types as Physics 1.

Free-response types
Mathematical routines, translation between representations, experimental design and analysis, and qualitative/quantitative translation.
Content
Thermodynamics, electric force, field and potential, circuits, magnetism and induction, geometric and wave optics, and modern physics.
Common weak spots
First-law signs, capacitors in circuits, ray-diagram conventions, and photon energy reasoning.

A Physics 2 thermodynamics habit

After a full cycle, ΔU is zero.

Your practice attempt

The net work is 900 J, so the net heat is 0.

What grading notices

The work is right. Over a full cycle ΔU = 0, so Q = W, not zero.

Luna's hint

What does the first law say when the gas returns to its starting state?
