Where BC points go missing

Series questions punish a missing condition more than a slow calculation.

A convergence test with an unchecked condition, an error bound applied to the wrong term, an Euler step that uses the new point's slope: BC questions are long, and one quiet slip carries through every later part.

The AP Calculus BC exam

AB content plus the BC topics, in the same format.

BC uses the same sections and timing as AB. Students also receive an AB subscore, so the AB skills count twice.

Multiple choice
42 questions in 1 hour 40 minutes: 29 without a calculator, then 13 with a graphing calculator. Half the score.
Free response
6 questions in 1 hour 30 minutes: 2 with a graphing calculator, 4 without. Half the score.
BC additions
Series and convergence, Taylor and Maclaurin polynomials with error bounds, Euler's method, logistic models, parametric, vector and polar functions.
Common weak spots
Unchecked test conditions, the wrong term in an error bound, interval endpoints, and polar area limits.
