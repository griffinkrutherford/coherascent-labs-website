Where Physics C points go missing

A Physics C derivation habit

A varying force needs an integral.

Your practice attempt

F(3) = 3 N, so W = F × d = 9 J.

What grading notices

The number happens to match here, but F × d assumes a constant force. The method has to be the integral.

Luna's hint

What is the integral of F(x) from 0 to 3 m?
