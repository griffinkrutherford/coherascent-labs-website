The calculator is not the problem

Most errors happen before the first keystroke.

CFA candidates rarely get a valuation wrong on the arithmetic. The mistake is upstream: a cash flow in the wrong period, an annual rate used with semiannual coupons, an argument about rates that moves the wrong curve. Writing the setup, or speaking it, shows which assumption failed.

The CFA Program

Three levels, from tools to portfolio decisions.

Each level is a computer-based exam taken in a testing window. The topics stay broadly the same, but each level asks more of them, from recall and calculation to analysis and portfolio judgment.

Level I
Multiple-choice questions on the investment tools: ethics, quantitative methods, economics, financial statement analysis, corporate issuers, equity, fixed income, derivatives, alternatives and portfolio management.
Level II
Item sets: a case vignette followed by several questions, focused on asset valuation and analysis.
Level III
Item sets and constructed-response questions on portfolio management, taken in a specialized pathway you choose.
Common weak spots
Cash flows placed in the wrong period, rate and compounding mismatches, and economics answers that shift the wrong curve.

Questions

CFA Program prep questions

Are these official CFA Institute questions?

No. Lune Synth writes its own practice questions and is not affiliated with CFA Institute. Use CFA Institute's curriculum and practice materials alongside it.

Which level is this best for?

Level I and Level II benefit most, because so much of both is setting up calculations and reading vignettes correctly. You choose the topic and difficulty for each practice set.

Can it check ethics answers?

It can give feedback on a written explanation of which standard applies and why. Treat CFA Institute's Standards of Practice Handbook as the authority.

Do I need a financial calculator?

You can work by hand, use your own calculator, or both. Lune Synth grades the setup and reasoning you write or speak, so you can see where a keystroke result came from.
