Turn biology knowledge into scored explanations.

AP Biology free response asks you to read data, design experiments and explain mechanisms in a sentence or two. Practice with pedigrees, gels and data on screen, and get feedback on the claim, the evidence and the reasoning you wrote.

Where AP Bio points go missing

The right answer without the mechanism earns partial credit.

Students often know the outcome but leave out the causal link: why water moves, why a carrier probability is 2/3, why a shorter fragment travels farther. Free response also asks for controls, variables and data interpretation, which flashcards do not practice.

The AP Biology exam

Data, experiments and written explanations.

A calculator and the equations and formulas sheet are allowed. Many questions give a graph, table or model and ask what it shows and why.

Multiple choice
60 questions in 1 hour 30 minutes, many built on data or a model. Half the score.
Free response
6 questions in 1 hour 30 minutes: 2 long questions and 4 short ones. Half the score.
What is scored
Explaining processes, analyzing models and data, statistical tests, experimental design, and argumentation.
Common weak spots
Identifying controls and variables, chi-square, conditional genetics probability, and answers missing the mechanism.

Questions shown are practice questions written for Lune Synth and rendered in the app, not official AP items. Exam formats are summarised from the College Board's course and exam descriptions; check AP Central for current details.

An AP Bio genetics habit

Condition on what you already know.

Your practice attempt

II-2 is a carrier with probability 1/2.

What grading notices

1/2 is the chance from an Aa × Aa cross before you know the phenotype. II-2 is unaffected, so aa is ruled out.

Luna's hint

Among the unaffected genotypes, what fraction are heterozygous?

Targeted next practice

Three pedigree probability questions with an unaffected sibling or offspring.
