Example feedback on a history argument

Your student's work

“The Boston Massacre caused the Stamp Act because the colonists were angry.” The order of the events is backwards.

What feedback points to

Connecting colonial anger to British policy is a good start. But the Stamp Act (1765) came before the Boston Massacre (1770), so it can't have been caused by it.

Luna's hint

Pick a British action on the timeline. What did the colonists do afterwards?

Next practice

Two more cause-and-effect questions about this part of the timeline.
