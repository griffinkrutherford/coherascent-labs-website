For families planning the summer

Keep the skills from June to September, a few minutes at a time.

Summer is long, and skills that go unused for ten weeks can feel rusty in September. A thick workbook is easy to abandon by July. Lune Synth keeps it small: a few-minute mission on one skill from last year, feedback on what your teen writes, and a map of next year's course to look ahead to.

Why summer plans fade

An hour a day is a plan. A few minutes is a habit.

Ambitious summer schedules tend to stop after the first trip or the first week of camp. Short sessions are easier to keep going. A Quick Mission takes a few minutes, a streak shows each day it happened, and a Constellation gives the summer a direction: last year's shaky skills first, then a preview of next year.

One summer session

Example feedback on a review question

Your teen's work

Drew a closed circle at 2, shaded to the right, and chose 2 as the whole number.

What feedback points to

Shading to the right is correct. x > 2 doesn't include 2 itself, so the circle should be open, and 2 can't be the answer to the second part.

Luna's hint

Is 2 greater than 2? What does that tell you about the circle and the whole number?

Next practice

Questions parents ask

Before you sign up

How much summer practice is enough?

There's no fixed amount, and your family knows the summer best. A Quick Mission takes a few minutes, so a short daily session is realistic even in a busy week.

Can we use next year's syllabus?

Yes. Your teen can describe a topic or attach a syllabus, notes or a reading list, and build practice or a Constellation from it.

Is it only for math?

No. Your teen can practise any subject they describe, from chemistry and history to Spanish or essay writing.

Is Lune Synth for my teen's age?

During the beta, Lune Synth accounts are for learners 13 and older, and a teen who is not yet an adult where you live uses it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Children under 13 can't create an account or join the waitlist on their own. The Terms and Privacy Policy have the details.
